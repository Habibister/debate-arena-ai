# 14 — Final Research Synthesis: Curriculum Draft V2 Decision Record

Status: **APPROVED decision record.** This is the concise standing reference for what the research
program decided and why. The full research reports (official-source passes, three Reddit passes, three
caption-analysis passes) and their correction addenda live in the session record and are **not** pasted

## 1. Evidence hierarchy

1. Current official governing sources · 2. Official instructional/page-description sources ·
3. Approved official-source synthesis findings (C/D/H) · 4. Repeated anecdotal findings (3+ independent
discussions) · 5. Isolated anecdotal findings · 6. Caption-analyzed examples · 7. Metadata-only or
retrieval-method findings · 8. Product hypotheses.

**Standing rules.** Official evidence controls rules, timing, scoring, safety, format, terminology and
event structure. Anecdote establishes recurring learner friction, never rules. Caption analysis supplies
examples, never general rules. Metadata-only and null passes establish no behavior. **A single asset may
never independently require a curriculum change. Absence of evidence is never converted into evidence of
absence. Provisional questions are never resolved by intuition.**

Full policy: doc 00 §2.

## 3. Final course-map summaries

**Debate** (doc 01) — 7 modules + Practice Lab. Module 1 gains a **flowing primer**, **first-tournament
orientation**, and a **watch-and-judge** capstone activity. Module 2 gains a **research lesson** and
splits evidence quality from **official evidence-rule literacy**. Module 3 splits judge adaptation into
**diagnosis** and **execution**. Module 4 is **compressed** with the repair capstone retained. Module 5
carries the crossfire correction, flexible collapse, and weighing sequencing. Module 7 leads with
**delivery diagnosis** — with **no numeric speaking-rate target anywhere**. Parliamentary is a flagged
placeholder only.

**DECA** (doc 02) — Navigator-first, family-routed. Module 0 orientation → Module 1 reading the
situation → Module 2 performing the meeting → Module 3 pressure and close (including **score-sheet
literacy**) → Module 4 family branches (Series / PBA / PFL / TDM) → Module 5 simulation. Prepared,
written and online families are **routed, not taught**.

**HOSA** (doc 03) — Navigator-first, then branch: **A** knowledge tests · **B** clinical-skill
communication · **C** interview events · **D** presentation events · **E** team orientation ·
**F** conference literacy. Branch B's **Compete stage is deferred pending advisor/judge validation, with
no hands-on simulation** — an explicit, documented exception to the universal progression.

## 8. Policy summary

**Source and freshness** (doc 00 §2–§4). Currency labels; the document's own date confirms currency,
never the URL; per-organization freshness calendars; **HOSA's final 2026-27 guidelines are expected
September 1, 2026 — one documented release date, not a permanent annual rule**; association and
competition-level variation is always named.

**Copyright and linking** (doc 00 §7). **Never host, reproduce, provide, or link to unauthorized copies.
Never route around payment, authentication, licensing, or access controls.** **Lawful links to official
publishers, authorized retailers, or library/school catalogs are permitted.** Teach from official
reference lists and test plans without copying protected content. Licensed or member-gated content is
never scraped.

**Linking versus embedding.** Linking to an official source is a different act from reproducing it.
**Embedding an official player requires a later rights, terms-of-use, technical and product review — no
research pass reached any legal conclusion about embedding.**

**Caption accessibility** (doc 00 §2). Every external asset carries a source-accessibility status.
**Required functionality must never depend on third-party caption availability.** Do not assume captions
will always be unavailable. **Never reconstruct a copyrighted transcript manually as a workaround.**

**Practice items** (doc 00 §6). Original · labeled AI-generated or CompeteReady-created · weighted only
from current official test plans · never copying official questions, paid references, textbooks or
third-party study sets · never presented as official, released or predictive.

**Medical safety** (doc 00 §8, doc 03 §7). CompeteReady **never teaches, scores, or simulates hands-on
clinical procedures**, never reproduces procedure steps, never evaluates clinical correctness, and
**never claims app practice creates clinical readiness.** Official distinctions, stated as HOSA's:
**live patients or actors may be used for noninvasive components where the current guideline and
equipment list specify them; invasive, medication, blood-draw, injection and resuscitation components use
appropriate manikins, training arms, medication trainers, or other specified simulation equipment; the
exact current event guideline and equipment list control.** **Supervision guidance is CompeteReady
policy, labeled as ours.**
