Read these, in order: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/CURRENT_STATE.md`, `docs/HANDOFF.md`, `docs/NEXT_TASK.md`.
`docs/CURRENT_STATE.md` and `docs/HANDOFF.md` are the authoritative current state; `docs/NEXT_TASK.md`
is subordinate to both and never overrides them. In the two canonical documents, only the region above
the historical-archive boundary is current.

Then state, in ~5 lines: current branch + latest commit, what works, what's blocked, and the next task
subject to those blockers.

Do not read the master plan or decisions unless the user asks. Do not scan the repo.
